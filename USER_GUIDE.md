# MindScroll — User Guide

MindScroll turns the best educational writing on the web into an endless, swipeable feed. Skim a hook, swipe to queue it, tap to read it in a clean reader, highlight what matters, and review it all later. This guide takes about five minutes.

---

## 1. Create your account

1. Open MindScroll and tap **Get started**.
2. Enter your name, email and a password (at least 8 characters).
3. You land on the topic picker straight away.

Everything you do — topics, saved articles, your reading queue, highlights and notes — belongs to your account alone. Nobody else can see it, and you can delete it permanently any time in **Settings**.

> **Tip:** On a phone, use your browser's **Add to Home Screen** option to launch MindScroll like an app.

---

## 2. Choose your topics

The **Topics** screen is a curated directory: Artificial Intelligence, Psychology, History, Science, Philosophy, Economics, Technology, Health & Medicine, Space & Astronomy, Environment & Climate, Business & Startups, Learning & Habits, Design & Creativity, and Literature & Culture.

* Tap a card to select or deselect it. Pick as many as you like (at least one).
* Each topic shows how many independent sources feed it. You never need to find or paste an RSS link.
* Tap **Start my feed** (or **Save topics** later) to build your feed.

You can change your selection at any time from the **Topics** tab.

**Why your feed feels varied:** inside a topic, MindScroll takes one article from Source A, then Source B, then Source C, and repeats — so no single publisher can take over. Topics are rotated against each other the same way.

---

## 3. Browse the infinite feed

The **Feed** tab shows one full-screen card at a time. Each card has a headline, a short 2–3 sentence teaser, tags, the source and a reading-time estimate (for example "⏱️ 2 min read").

| Action | Phone | Desktop |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Next / previous card | Swipe or scroll up / down | Mouse wheel, ↓ ↑ buttons, `↓` / `j` and `↑` / `k` |
| Open the reader | Tap the card | Click the card, or `Enter` / `o` |
| Add to **Read Later** | **Swipe the card left** or tap the clock icon | Drag left, click the clock icon, or press `l` |
| **Save** to your library | Tap the bookmark icon | Click the bookmark, or press `s` |
| Open the original site | Tap the arrow-out icon | Same |

The row of chips at the top (**For you**, then each of your topics) filters the feed. Choose a single topic to see only that topic, still blended across its sources.

### The "never again" rule

If you scroll past a card without saving it or adding it to Read Later, MindScroll marks it as seen and **it will never appear in your feed again**. Cards you swiped to Read Later or saved stay in their libraries. When you have seen everything for now, you get an "all caught up" message — fresh articles are fetched throughout the day.

Swiped by accident? Tap **Undo** in the message that appears.

---

## 4. Reading mode

Tap a card and it expands smoothly into a full-screen, distraction-free reader: no ads, menus or clutter, just the article text and images. Swipe back, press the back button, or press `Esc` to return to exactly where you were in the feed.

### Toolbar

Scroll down and the toolbar tucks away; scroll up (or tap the text) to bring it back. A thin progress bar at the top shows how far you have read, and MindScroll remembers your position in each article.

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| ← | Back to the feed or list you came from |
| **T** (Aa) | Opens the appearance panel (below) |
| ☀ / ☾ | Quickly cycles through the themes |
| 🕒 | Adds or removes the article from Read Later |
| 🔖 | Saves or unsaves the article |

### Themes (Moon+ Reader style)

Open the **T** (Aa) button to choose a theme:

* **OLED Black** — pure `#000000` background for AMOLED screens and night reading
* **Midnight** — deep slate `#0f172a`, easy on the eyes
* **Sepia** — warm paper `#f4ecd8`
* **Classic Light** — crisp white `#ffffff`
* **Forest Mint** — gentle green tint `#e8f5e9`

### Typography

In the same panel:

* **Text size** — slider or − / + buttons (14–30 px)
* **Line spacing** — slider or − / + buttons (1.3–2.4)
* **Typeface** — Serif or Sans-serif
* **Reset** (circular arrow) restores the defaults

Your choices apply instantly, are remembered on your device, and follow you to other devices when you sign in.

## 5. Highlights and notes

1. In the reader, **press and hold** a word (touch) or **click and drag** (mouse), then adjust the selection.
2. A bar appears at the bottom with four colours — yellow, green, blue, pink. Tap a colour to highlight the passage.
3. Tap the **note** button (speech-bubble with a plus) to highlight *and* type a note straight away.
4. **Tap any highlight** to reopen it: edit the note, change the colour, or remove it.

Highlights with a note carry a small underline so you can spot them at a glance. A new highlight cannot overlap an existing one — tap the existing highlight to edit it instead. The bar also has **Copy** and a close button.

---

## 6. Your libraries

Three separate places, all private to you. Use the bottom bar (phone) or the sidebar (desktop).

### Read Later

A queue for quick access to articles you swiped or queued. Each row shows the source and reading time. Tap a row to read it. The bookmark button **moves** an article to Saved; the trash button removes it from the queue (with Undo). A search box filters the list.

### Saved

Your permanent library of articles worth keeping. Tap to read, remove with the trash button, or push an article back to Read Later.

### Highlights & Notes (the vault)

A completely separate dashboard that gathers every highlight and note, **grouped by article**, newest first — whether or not the article is saved. Here you can:

* **Search** across highlights, notes and article titles
* **Add or edit a note** on any highlight, or delete a highlight
* **Open** the source article from its group header
* **Export Markdown** to take your highlights and notes into other tools

---

## 7. Settings and privacy

Open **Settings** (gear icon on phones; bottom of the sidebar on desktop) to:

* manage topics,
* export your highlights and notes,
* sign out,
* **delete your account** — permanently removes your profile, libraries, highlights and notes.

---

## 8. Quick answers

**An article shows "couldn't extract a clean version."** Some publishers block readers or require a subscription. You can still open the original with the **Original** button.

**The feed says I'm all caught up.** You have seen everything currently available for your topics. Add more topics for more variety, or check back later.

**Can I get a dismissed article back?** Dismissed cards are intentionally gone for good. If something might be worth revisiting, swipe left (Read Later) or tap the bookmark before you scroll on.

**Keyboard shortcuts (desktop):** `↓`/`j` next · `↑`/`k` previous · `Enter`/`o` open · `l` Read Later · `s` Save · `Esc` close the reader.
